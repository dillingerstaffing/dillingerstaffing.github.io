Why a folio, not a link list. A list asks nothing of the visitor. A folio states its conclusion first (start with the site that matches your question), keys each claim to its proof, and keeps its revisions visible. This page is the index demonstrating the language it indexes.

01 · The working sheet

Every site, in three states of certainty.

The language assigns graphic weight to the status of a thought, not color to a topic. Committed sites are settled and kept. Working sites are under active revision. Underdrawing holds the next folio, still allowed to be wrong. Nine sites across three registers.

Ordering premise, changes what the sheet compares

Commitment layer, isolates one state of certainty

New benches graduate into portfolio proof cards. The lab feeds the record.
This sheet is set in the Notebook design language it indexes.

premise / folio order · 9 folios on the sheet

Direct keying. Each margin note touches the claim it qualifies. The sanguine leaders end at the exact blocks they describe, recalculated with the rendered layout. No detached legend.

Answer-first. The conclusion (which site matches your question) is visible before any control is touched. The controls change the comparison, never gate the answer.

Small multiples. Every block shares one format. Only the state of certainty changes, so comparison happens in space, not from memory.

02 · The register

Why each site deserves your visit.

One subject, one verdict. The line in ink is the reason to go; the disclosure beside it carries the evidence. Nothing here asks you to take the verdict on faith.
